What This Tool Does

It compares all 311 passenger aircraft available in the AM4 order menu and calculates the most profitable configuration for your route.

Fleet Mode

There are two calculation modes:

Single Aircraft

Finds the optimal aircraft type and its seat configuration.

Multi Aircraft

Uses a greedy algorithm that builds the fleet step by step: it iteratively selects the most profitable aircraft for the remaining demand, subtracts its transported passengers from the remaining demand, and repeats until either all demand is served or no additional aircraft would be profitable (max. 30 aircraft).

The result is displayed as a purple card showing the full fleet composition with demand coverage bars (Y J F), grouped by type with quantity, and a profit total row.

Route Parameters

Origin & Destination

For origin and destination, pick a Country (all countries in the game, alphabetical) and then an Airport in that country (alphabetical, with IATA code). The airport list becomes active once a country is selected. The destination cannot be the same airport as the origin.

Once both airports are selected, the tool fills in the following fields from the game data (highlighted green). All values can still be overwritten — and the fields can also be filled in manually without any selection.

  • Distance (km) — great-circle distance between the two airports, calculated as in the game
  • Max. Runway (ft) — the shorter of the two airports' maximum runway lengths
  • Y / J / F Demand — route demand Y J F according to the game data

Field Reference

  • Distance (km) — Distance between origin and destination
  • Max. Runway (ft) — Shortest runway on the route. Any aircraft type whose minimum runway requirement exceeds this value is automatically excluded.
  • Y Demand / Day — Daily demand for Economy seats (Y)
  • J Demand / Day — Daily demand for Business seats (J)
  • F Demand / Day — Daily demand for First Class seats (F)

Click "Calculate" and the green card shows the optimal type with its configuration and return. You can filter by Short-/Medium-/Long-Haul and sort by any column.

Costs & Settings

Adjust costs according to your situation:

  • Fuel — $/1000 lbs (default = 500)
  • CO₂ Quota — $/unit (default = 140)
  • Period — Days used for calculation (default = 20)
  • Flight Hours / Day — Per aircraft (default = 20)
  • Market Rate Sell Location — Percentage the market at the aircraft's current location pays (default = 35%). This value can be found in-game under "Sell" for each aircraft.
  • Pilots — $/day (default = 200). Salaries can be found in the game under "Airline" → "Staff"; they change with pay raises and cuts.
  • Crew — $/day (default = 150)
  • Engineers — $/day (default = 250)
  • Technicians — $/day (default = 225)
  • Game Mode — Easy or Realism (default = Realism)
  • Fleet Mode — See Single Aircraft and Multi Aircraft

Results

Departures per Day

In AM4, the flight time of a departure is the one-way trip: Flight Time = Distance / Speed. In Easy mode, aircraft fly 1.5× faster. Departures per day are Flight Hours per Day / Flight Time (rounded down). If the flight time exceeds the flight hours per day, it is calculated proportionally: with 20 flight hours and a 25-hour flight time, that is 0.8 departures per day, and revenue and flight costs are scaled to a daily figure accordingly.

Revenue

Ticket prices follow the official AM4 formulas (Easy/Realism selectable), with optimal multipliers (Y ×1.1 J ×1.08 F ×1.06). Seat configuration is automatically optimized by F>J>Y priority based on the entered demand. As in the game, the configuration always uses the aircraft's full capacity: seats without demand are assigned to economy and shown as unsold, e.g. "250 Y (184 requested, 66 unsold)". Revenue is only earned for requested seats.

Costs

  • Fuel — Distance × consumption (lbs/km) × price
  • CO₂ Quota — Distance × CO₂ coefficient × price
  • Staff costs — Daily salaries × crew size of the aircraft type
  • A-check maintenance — Amortized over the calculation period. In Realism mode an A-check costs twice as much as in Easy mode (as shown in the game).

Depreciation

The tool uses the official AM4 resale formula in two steps:

1. Value reduction by flight hours:

Current Value = max(Purchase Price − 2,500 × Flight Hours, 10% × Purchase Price)

At 180 days × 20h/day plus 35h from factory = 3,635 flight hours.

2. Market rate at sell location:

Sale Price = Current Value × Market %

The market percentage varies by airport where the aircraft is located (typically 30–40%).

Depreciation per day:

(Purchase Price − Sale Price) / 180 days

The daily share is proportionally included in the costs for the selected calculation period.

For expensive aircraft like the Concorde ($408M), depreciation has a massive impact, while cheaper types like the B737-800 ($4.4M) are barely affected.

Daily Profit and ROI

Both include all costs including depreciation. The result is a ranking by daily profit and ROI (profit / purchase price), with the winner highlighted.
